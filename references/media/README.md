# `references/media/` — third-party reference material

**Nothing in this directory is the work of this project, and the repository's
[MIT license](../../LICENSE) does not cover it.**

These are ten infographics collected as source material while the pipeline was being designed (#0).
They are retained because the reasoning that came out of them is cited throughout
[`../notes-transcriptions/notes.md`](../notes-transcriptions/notes.md) — its "Source material — what
to steal from each" section reads them one by one, and several decisions trace directly to that
reading: the per-stage guiding question is **#44**, and budget plus the communication plan entering
stage 9 is **#17**, caught by testing the nine stages against a ten-step checklist in one of them.

Removing the images would leave those citations pointing at nothing. Keeping them means being
explicit about what they are.

## What is claimed, and what is not

- **No copyright is claimed** over any file here. Each belongs to its original author.
- **The MIT grant does not extend to them.** Cloning or forking this repository conveys no right to
  redistribute or reuse these images. If you fork it and intend to publish, remove this directory.
- They are held as **reference for design commentary and criticism**, not redistributed as a
  collection, and none is used as an asset by the application or the handoff package.
- Nothing here is doctrine. `notes.md` treats them as *vocabulary* — layout patterns and checklists
  to test the pipeline against — and disagrees with them where it has reason to.

## What is here

Four are identified and discussed by name in `notes.md`:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `The Three Layers of Planning.jpg` | Strategic / tactical / operational, as card-with-colored-spine layout. |
| `How to write a project plan.jpg` | A ten-step numbered grid — the checklist the nine stages were tested against. |
| `Project Management Documentation.jpg` | A branching template tree; effectively a menu of ~40 document types. |
| `Project Management Chart 1.jpg` | A radial mind-map. Weakest on content; kept for the layout. |

The remaining six are hash-named and **their provenance was never recorded**. They are undocumented
in `notes.md` and nothing in the project cites them. If this directory is ever pruned, they are the
files to prune first — an image whose source nobody wrote down cannot be attributed, and an
unattributable image is the one least defensible to keep.
